MyMedia

Your photos and videos, organized in your Google Drive

MyMedia is a photo and video organizer that works directly on your own Google Drive. Your files stay in your Drive, in ordinary folders you can open anywhere. MyMedia has no photo storage of its own.

Open MyMedia

What MyMedia does

Categories and albums

A "MyMedia" folder in your Drive, with categories (Family, Birds, Work…) and albums inside. Moving a photo moves the file in Drive.

Upload with the real dates

From your phone, computer, USB stick or SD card. The shooting date is kept (EXIF, video date or file name), and the files can be compressed to save space.

Find and clean

Duplicates, blurry photos, search by name, text, album or person.

Faces and automatic sorting

Recognizes the people in your photos and suggests the right album. The analysis runs on your device.

Share with family

Give read-only access to your library to the Google accounts you choose.

On every device

Install it from the browser on Android, iPhone, Windows and Mac, or use the Android app with its Share menu.

Why MyMedia asks for access to your Google Drive

When you sign in, Google asks you to let MyMedia see, edit, create and delete your Google Drive files. MyMedia needs this to:

  • read the photos and videos already in your MyMedia folder, including the ones you added outside MyMedia (Drive website, phone backup);
  • create the category and album folders, upload your photos and videos, and move, rename or delete them when you ask;
  • save the information about your photos (texts, people, albums) in files of your MyMedia folder, so that all your devices see it;
  • import photos from other Drive folders you choose, and open the MyMedia libraries other people share with you.

Your photos are never copied to MyMedia's servers, never sold and never used for advertising. Details in the privacy policy.

Price

MyMedia is free. MyMedia Pro (36 ₪ per year, or 50 ₪ once for life, paid with PayPal) adds the duplicate and blurry photo tools, sharing with more than 2 people and the transfer of a library to another account.

Pourquoi MyMedia demande l'accès à votre Google Drive

À la connexion, Google vous demande d'autoriser MyMedia à voir, modifier, créer et supprimer les fichiers de votre Google Drive. MyMedia en a besoin pour :

  • lire les photos et vidéos déjà dans votre dossier MyMedia, y compris celles ajoutées sans MyMedia (site Drive, sauvegarde du téléphone) ;
  • créer les dossiers des catégories et albums, envoyer vos photos et vidéos, et les déplacer, renommer ou supprimer quand vous le demandez ;
  • enregistrer les informations de vos photos (textes, personnes, albums) dans des fichiers de votre dossier MyMedia, pour que tous vos appareils les voient ;
  • importer des photos d'autres dossiers Drive que vous choisissez, et ouvrir les bibliothèques MyMedia que d'autres personnes partagent avec vous.

Vos photos ne sont jamais copiées sur les serveurs de MyMedia, jamais vendues et jamais utilisées pour de la publicité. Détails dans la politique de confidentialité.
